How It Works

A staged process from unused data to clearer decisions.

You do not need to start with a dashboard. We begin by understanding what data you already have, whether it can be trusted, and what decisions it should support.

Three connected stages

Data Assessment Report

Who it is for

Organisations with data in spreadsheets, CRM systems, dashboards, reports, or website analytics, but no clear view of what it means or what to trust.

What you get

  • review of current data sources
  • data quality and gap assessment
  • stakeholder conversations where needed
  • short report with recommendations
  • clear next-step roadmap

Outcome

You know what data you have, what is useful, what is missing, and what should happen next.

Market, Metrics & Behavioural Insight

Who it is for

Businesses that need richer data, clearer customer understanding, better metrics, or market/category insight before making growth decisions.

What you get

  • market/category research where useful
  • behavioural interpretation of customers or teams
  • new metrics or intentional data collection
  • website, positioning, or performance recommendations
  • clearer hypotheses to test

Outcome

You know what to measure next, what changes to test, and where growth or performance improvement may come from.

Behavioural Data System & Dashboard

Who it is for

Leaders who need ongoing tracking, dashboards, intervention measurement, or evidence of change over time.

What you get

  • custom dashboard or system design
  • repeated measurement structure
  • performance or behaviour tracking
  • insight panels and interpretation
  • maintenance or ongoing review where needed

Outcome

You get a decision-support system that tracks what changes and helps you decide what to improve next.

The Process

The 9-step process supports the journey from assessment to insight to systems, so dashboards only happen when they are the useful next step.

1

Start with the survey

Estimated time: 2–3 minutes

Complete a short Data Use & Performance Survey to reflect on how your organisation currently uses data.

2

Receive a follow-up

Estimated time: within 24–48 hours

We review your responses and, where appropriate, send a short summary with a link to book a free 15-minute consultation.

3

Free 15-minute consultation

Estimated time: 15 minutes

We discuss your results, your goals, and whether a deeper Data Clarity Assessment is useful.

4

Data access and confidentiality

Estimated time: 1–3 days

We agree what data can be reviewed, whether an NDA is needed, and whether any sensitive data is involved.

5

Data source review

Estimated time: 3–5 working days

We review existing sources such as spreadsheets, CRM exports, website analytics, survey data, reports, and dashboards.

6

Data quality and gap assessment

Estimated time: 3–5 working days

We assess completeness, consistency, reliability, missing fields, duplication, accessibility, and usefulness for decision-making.

7

Intentional data collection or additional metrics

Estimated time: optional, 1–2 weeks

If important information is missing, we recommend additional metrics, survey questions, or custom scientific measures.

8

Report and recommendations

Estimated time: 1 week

You receive a clear report showing current data strengths, gaps, risks, missed opportunities, and recommended next steps.

9

Build, test, and track

Estimated time: project-dependent

If useful, we move on to dashboards, market insight, tracking systems, or ongoing measurement that supports better decisions.

Optional Add-ons

  • Custom scientific measures
  • Market / category research
  • Dashboard education
  • Ongoing insight review
  • Funnel / method testing
