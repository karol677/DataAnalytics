How It Works

From messy data to clearer decisions — a simple staged process.

How It Works

We start by understanding your current data before recommending dashboards, reports, or systems. The process is designed to be clear, staged, and easy to follow.

The Process

1

Start with the survey

A short survey helps identify how your organisation currently uses data and where the main gaps may be.

2

Receive a follow-up

We review your answers and, where appropriate, send a short summary within 24–48 hours, along with a link to book a free 15-minute consultation.

3

Free 15-minute consultation

We discuss your results, your goals, and whether a deeper data assessment is the right next step.

4

Data Assessment

We review your current data sources, assess quality and structure, and speak with key people where needed.

5

Short report and recommendations

You receive a clear overview of your current data state, key gaps, and what should happen next.

6

Improve and interpret

If needed, we help improve data quality, infrastructure, and the way data is used for performance decisions.

7

Build what is needed

If a dashboard or ongoing system is appropriate, we build it around your actual needs.

8

Ongoing optimisation

Where appropriate, we can continue with reporting, dashboard upkeep, and testing different methods or funnels.

Optional Add-ons

  • Custom scientific measures
  • Market / category research
  • Dashboard education
  • Ongoing insight review
  • Funnel / method testing

Simple, staged, and practical

You do not need to commit to a complex build straight away. We begin with clarity, then recommend the right next step based on evidence.
