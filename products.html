      Relevant Experience & Project Work

      Across previous and ongoing work, E Analytics Studio draws on experience across data assessment, survey design, behavioural analysis, reporting, market insight, dashboards, and applied research.

      Data Assessment & Data Quality

      • Reviewing existing data sources across spreadsheets, dashboards, surveys, CRM exports, operational records, and performance reports
      • Assessing data completeness, consistency, accessibility, reliability, duplication, and missing fields
      • Identifying whether existing data is suitable for decision-making, reporting, or dashboard development
      • Recommending improvements to data structure, collection processes, and reporting systems

      Survey Design, Testing & Validation

      • Designing and refining behavioural and organisational survey measures
      • Testing questionnaire clarity, usability, structure, and completion burden
      • Shortening survey tools while preserving diagnostic value
      • Conducting user experience testing for survey completion
      • Producing evidence summaries to support credibility and client-facing use

      Behavioural & Performance Analysis

      • Analysing behavioural patterns linked to team performance, communication, customer interaction, and operational outcomes
      • Connecting performance data with human factors such as decision-making, motivation, trust, confidence, communication, and consistency
      • Supporting intervention planning through data-informed behavioural insight
      • Measuring whether changes, training, or interventions produce observable improvements

      Reporting, Insight & Communication

      • Producing clear reports covering methods, results, insights, and recommendations
      • Translating complex data into plain-English summaries for non-technical stakeholders
      • Creating tables, charts, and visual summaries for leadership, professional groups, and organisational audiences
      • Preparing findings for presentations, conferences, internal reports, and publication-style outputs

      Market, Category & Growth Research

      • Reviewing market/category opportunities for growth-focused businesses
      • Analysing potential customer segments and ICP expansion opportunities
      • Connecting internal data with external market insight
      • Identifying website, positioning, marketing, or product changes that can be tested and tracked
      • Producing recommendations linked to growth, investment priorities, and potential ROI

      Dashboard & Data System Development

      • Supporting the design of dashboards for performance, impact, team, and organisational reporting
      • Translating stakeholder needs into trackable data points and dashboard features
      • Working with developers and stakeholders to shape dashboard requirements
      • Creating data structures that allow performance, change, and impact to be monitored over time
      • Building the foundations for ongoing data systems rather than one-off reports

      Research & Evaluation

      • Designing and analysing applied behavioural research projects
      • Supporting observational, pre-post, survey-based, and intervention evaluation work
      • Cleaning, structuring, and analysing data across multiple research contexts
      • Producing brief reports, technical summaries, and scientific outputs
      • Applying research methods to real-world organisational and performance questions
