I’m Dr Karol Nedza, and I run E Analytics Studio as a founder-led practice. My work sits between behavioural science and analytics—where measurement meets real decisions, and where most systems quietly break down.

Across sport, health, education, and corporate performance, I’ve seen the same pattern: organisations collect data, but struggle to translate it into clarity. The issue is rarely a lack of numbers. It’s the gap between what is measured, what is trusted, and what people can realistically do with the results.

E Analytics Studio exists to close that gap—carefully, ethically, and with a commitment to evidence over noise.

Values & Ethos

  • Integrity in data — measurement should be defensible, traceable, and handled with care.
  • Evidence over opinion — decisions improve when claims are testable and assumptions are visible.
  • Clarity over complexity — analysis should simplify reality without distorting it.
  • Behaviour matters — numbers describe patterns; behaviour explains why patterns hold or change.
  • Long-term thinking — sustainable performance comes from systems that remain useful over time.

Services

When data exists but decisions don’t improve

You may have dashboards or reports—yet the same questions keep returning, and decisions default to instinct. I align data to the decisions it must support, reduce noise, and rebuild a measurement spine leaders can rely on.

When teams collect metrics but don’t trust them

If definitions shift, sources conflict, or results can’t be reproduced, confidence erodes. I strengthen data foundations, clarify definitions, improve comparability over time, and document what “good” looks like.

When behavioural factors are ignored in analysis

Performance is shaped by attention, incentives, habits, friction, and culture. I integrate behavioural insight so findings reflect real-world outcomes, not just statistical movement.

When leaders lack a clear picture over time

Without consistent measurement, it’s difficult to see whether changes are working—or merely fluctuating. I build reporting that highlights trends, stability, uncertainty, and meaningful change.
