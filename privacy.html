Privacy notice

Last updated: September 2026

This notice explains what E Analytics Studio collects through this website, why, how long it is kept and how to have it removed. It is written to be read rather than to be legally impressive.

Who we are

E Analytics Studio, Plymouth, United Kingdom. The data controller is Dr Karol Nedza. You can reach us at karol@eanalyticsstudio.com for any question about your data, including deletion.

What we collect, and why

WhatWhenWhy
Name, email, company If you request the written interpretation of your diagnostic result, or use the contact form To send you what you asked for, and to reply
Your diagnostic answers and scores Only if you submit them with the form To produce the written interpretation. Your answers are not sent anywhere if you simply read your scores on screen
Anonymous page statistics Every visit To understand how many people read which pages. No cookies and no individual identification

You can complete the diagnostic and read your scores without giving us anything. Data is only sent if you fill in the form and tick the consent box.

Lawful basis

For the diagnostic interpretation and any follow-up about it, we rely on your consent, given by ticking the box on the form. For replies to the contact form, we rely on legitimate interests, specifically responding to an enquiry you sent us.

Who else can see it

Form submissions are stored in Google Sheets, so Google acts as a processor on our behalf. Email is handled through our email provider. We do not sell your data, we do not share it with advertisers, and we do not add you to any mailing list you did not ask for.

How long we keep it

  • Diagnostic submissions and enquiry details: 24 months, then deleted.
  • Email correspondence: for as long as the business relationship is live, then 24 months.
  • Anonymous page statistics: retained in aggregate, with no way to identify you.

Cookies

This site sets no advertising or tracking cookies and shows no cookie banner, because it does not need one. Page statistics are collected without cookies and without identifying individual visitors.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, ask us to delete it, or withdraw consent at any time. Email karol@eanalyticsstudio.com and we will act within one month, usually much sooner.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes

If this notice changes, the date at the top changes with it. Material changes affecting people whose data we already hold will be communicated directly.
