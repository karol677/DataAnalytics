The method

Behavioural impact, defined.

Impact is not a feeling about whether something went well. It is a quantity, and it has three parts. Miss any one of them and the number you report does not mean what you think it means.

b₂ − b₁

The measured difference on the behaviour that matters. Requires a before, which is the one component that cannot be recovered after the fact.

r · repetition

How often it recurs, across how many people, and for how long it holds. A change that happens once is an anecdote. A change that repeats is a result.

e · effort

What it took to produce. A small change at trivial cost can be worth more than a large one that consumed a year.

The problem

Everyone measures what was delivered. Almost nobody measures what changed.

The same failure in three costumes. In each case what gets counted is the effort, not the effect.

Product

You shipped the feature

Usage is up and funnel metrics moved. Whether people behave differently, or a number responded to something else entirely, is unresolved.

Missing: did behaviour change, or did a metric?
Platform

You rolled out the platform

Licences issued, dashboards built, governance published. Nobody can show that a single decision is now made differently because of it.

Missing: does anyone work or decide differently?
Programme

You delivered the programme

Attendance, completion and satisfaction all look healthy. None of it answers the question the person who approved the budget is asking.

Missing: is anything different afterwards?

The data industry sells transformation and measures installation. Adoption is the most under-measured thing in it, and adoption is a behaviour problem wearing a technical costume.

What we evaluate

Three things can be evaluated.

Level 01

A product or feature

Something you shipped. Did it change what users actually do, or did a metric move while behaviour stayed where it was?

Level 02

A project or programme

Something delivered to people over time. Training, onboarding, a service change, a funded intervention. Did it hold, and for whom?

Level 03

A team

How a team decides, challenges and acts on what it knows. The most under-measured thing in any data-rich organisation.
