Data Use & Performance Survey

This free diagnostic is designed to help you understand how your organisation currently collects, understands, and uses data.

The questions are intentionally focused on three areas:

  • whether you know where your data lives
  • whether you understand and trust the data you collect
  • whether your data supports performance decisions

Your responses can help identify whether your organisation has a data clarity problem, a data quality problem, or a data use problem.

The survey was designed by Dr Karol Nedza, a behavioural scientist and data strategist with a PhD in Psychology and experience in research methods, survey design, and applied data analysis.

It takes around 2-3 minutes. If you ask for a review, E Analytics Studio may follow up with a short interpretation of your results.
