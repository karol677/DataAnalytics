Team Assessment Package

Understand your team's performance drivers and unlock its potential.

What Is Team Assessment?

The Team Assessment Package combines scientifically-led assessment methods with data-driven analysis to help you understand how your team performs. Rather than relying on generic personality frameworks, this assessment focuses on behaviours that directly impact performance.

We assess individual contributions, team dynamics, and the systems that enable performance—giving you actionable insights rather than abstract profiles.

What You Get

Individual Insights

Each team member receives a detailed assessment report covering their approach to work, decision-making patterns, and collaboration style. The focus is on behaviours that drive performance.

Team Dashboard

Leaders gain a comprehensive view of team composition, strengths, and potential areas for improvement. Visualisations show how individual differences combine to create team dynamics.

Behavioural Analysis

Understand communication patterns, decision-making approaches, and conflict resolution styles within your team. This helps identify where collaboration naturally flows and where friction might arise.

Leader Dashboard

Monitor team performance over time, track development priorities, and measure the impact of interventions. The dashboard updates as team composition or performance shifts.

The Assessment Process

Our assessment is designed to be simple and insightful, not burdensome.

Step 1: Individual Assessment

Team members complete a brief assessment (15-20 minutes) exploring their work approach, decision-making style, and teamwork preferences.

Step 2: Data Analysis

We analyse the results to identify team composition, strengths, and areas where team dynamics might need support.

Step 3: Insights & Dashboards

Team members receive their individual reports. Leaders access the team dashboard and leader portal for ongoing performance tracking.

Why This Matters

  • Reduce Friction: Understand communication styles and potential conflict points before they impact work.
  • Improve Collaboration: Build on natural strengths rather than trying to change fundamental work styles.
  • Data-Driven Development: Know exactly where to invest in team development for maximum impact.
  • Sustainable Performance: Create systems and practices that work with your team's composition, not against it.
