From Data to Decision

More data does not create better decisions. Better understanding does.

I created E Analytics Studio because the same problem kept appearing in growing, people-powered businesses: data was everywhere, but clarity was missing.

As your business grows, data spreads across spreadsheets, software, staff updates, bookings, customers, surveys, reports, and dashboards. The result is often more noise, slower decisions, and less confidence in what is working.

The thesis explains why this happens, why dashboards alone rarely fix it, and how businesses can move from scattered data to clearer action.

How E Analytics Studio helps

A simple three-step process for turning scattered business data into clearer decisions.

Step 1 · Organise

Understand Your Data

We assess your data infrastructure — every source and format — and work out what's comparable, duplicated, or missing. This is how fragmentation gets solved first.

Who it is for

For businesses with data in spreadsheets, CRM systems, dashboards, reports, booking tools, surveys, or website analytics, but no shared view of what it means or what can be trusted.

What you get

  • Review of current data sources and data flow
  • Data quality, infrastructure, and gap assessment
  • Trusted metrics and decision priorities
  • Stakeholder conversations where needed
  • Clear next-step roadmap

You know what data exists, what can be trusted, what is missing, and what to fix first.

Step 2 · Understand

Identify Behavioural Insights

We identify the gaps and recommend how to fix them — then bring in everyone with a stake in the data, often through short staff interviews, so what we build reflects how the work really happens.

Who it is for

For businesses that need to understand customers, teams, service performance, operational patterns, or market signals before making growth or improvement decisions.

What you get

  • Behavioural interpretation of customers, teams, or service patterns
  • Market or category research where useful
  • New metrics or intentional data collection
  • Website, positioning, service, or performance recommendations
  • Clearer hypotheses to test

You know what the patterns suggest, what to measure next, what changes to test, and where improvement may come from.

Step 3 · Act

Build Decision Systems

We build the sustainable solution that fits: a CRM, a live dashboard, or a dynamic report your team and clients can check every week.

Who it is for

For leaders who need ongoing tracking, evidence of change, intervention measurement, or a clearer way to review decisions over time.

What you get

  • Custom dashboard or decision-system design
  • Repeated measurement structure
  • Performance, behaviour, or impact tracking
  • Insight panels and interpretation
  • Maintenance or ongoing review where needed

You get a decision-support system that keeps evidence visible, tracks what changes, and helps you decide what to improve next.

About Karol

Dr Karol Nedza founded E Analytics Studio to help founder-led, people-powered businesses move from data collection to better decisions. The company combines PhD-level behavioural science, applied data analysis, decision support, and practical technology.

Karol has taught psychology, research methods, and data analysis for nearly a decade, and has delivered applied research and consultancy projects across healthcare, education, business, and performance settings.

His strength is translating complex data and human behaviour into clear business choices: what to trust, what to fix, what to measure next, and what action to take.

Outside of work, Karol is driven by movement, challenge, and curiosity, from coastal running and surfing to writing about modern life, identity, and human behaviour. That human lens is central to the work: better decisions come from understanding both the data and the people behind it.
